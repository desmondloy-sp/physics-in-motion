Power and Efficiency of a Lifting Machine

Work done = GPE gained = mgh
—
Useful power output = mgh/t
—
Power input = output ÷ efficiency
—
Power wasted (heat, sound)
—
Lifting power right now = mgv
—
Run the motor — or grab the load and lift it yourself. Lift faster and see your power go up.
Efficiency = useful energy output ÷ total energy input × 100% (= useful power output ÷ power input × 100%). The motor must take in more energy than the load gains; the difference is wasted, mostly as heat and sound. Power is the rate of doing work, P = W/t: the same lift done in less time needs more power. g = 10 m s-2.
