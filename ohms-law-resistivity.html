Drag the crocodile clip along the wire to change the length L in the circuit.
  • The ammeter is in series with the wire. The voltmeter is connected in parallel across the length of wire being tested.
  • R = ρL/A: a longer wire has more resistance, a thicker wire (bigger A) has less, and a material with a higher resistivity has more.
  • Low-resistivity metals (silver, copper) make good connecting wires. High-resistivity nichrome and tungsten are used in heating elements and filaments.
