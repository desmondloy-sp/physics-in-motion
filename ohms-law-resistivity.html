Set a voltage and press Record this reading. Repeat at several voltages, then choose the other component.
  • The ammeter is in series with the component. The voltmeter is connected in parallel across it.
  • Ohm's law: the current I is directly proportional to the voltage V, provided the physical conditions (e.g. temperature) of the conductor stay the same.
  • The lamp is rated 3.0 V, so the supply stops at 3.0 V (Tutorial B7).
  • The ammeter is in series with the wire. The voltmeter is connected in parallel across the length of wire being tested.
  • R = ρL/A: a longer wire has more resistance, a thicker wire (bigger A) has less, and a material with a higher resistivity has more.
  • Low-resistivity metals (silver, copper) make good connecting wires. High-resistivity nichrome and tungsten are used in heating elements and filaments.
