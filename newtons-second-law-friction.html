Press Start for a 10 s run. Grab the red handle on the force arrow and drag it left or right to push or pull the block, before the run or while it moves.
  • Friction always opposes motion.
  • While the block is at rest, friction matches your push, up to its limit f. The forces stay balanced and the block stays at rest (first law).
  • Push harder than f and there is a net force, so the block accelerates in the direction of the net force (second law).
  • If the push exactly equals friction while the block is moving, the net force is zero, so it carries on at constant velocity.
