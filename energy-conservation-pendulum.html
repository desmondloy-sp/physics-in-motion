Grab the bob, pull it to one side and let go.
With air resistance off, the total mechanical energy (GPE + KE) stays constant — energy just converts back and forth between the two forms. Switch on air resistance to see the total mechanical energy slowly fall as it is lost to heat, and the swing's amplitude shrink. Energy is not destroyed: the total energy, including the heat, stays the same.
