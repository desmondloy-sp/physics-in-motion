Drag the arrowheads of F1, F2 and F3. Can you make the chain on the right close up? Try it yourself before you press Show the answer.
  • Left: the three forces acting at one point on the object. Right: the same forces drawn tip to tail.
  • When ΣFx = 0 and ΣFy = 0, the chain closes into a triangle and the net force is zero.
  • The object is then in equilibrium: it stays at rest, or keeps moving at constant velocity (Newton's first law).
  • If the chain does not close, the red arrow from the start to the end of the chain is the net force left over.
  • The balancing force that would restore equilibrium is equal in size and opposite in direction to that net force.
