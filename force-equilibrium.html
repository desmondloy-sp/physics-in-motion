When the net force is zero
  • ΣFx = 0 and ΣFy = 0, so the connecting arrows (the forces drawn tip to tail) close into a triangle.
  • The object is in equilibrium: it stays at rest, or keeps moving at constant velocity (Newton's first law).
When the net force is not zero
  • The connecting arrows do not close. The red arrow from the start to the end represents the net force.
  • The balancing force that would restore equilibrium is equal in size and opposite in direction to that net force.
