Work done by each force so far. Bars above the line are positive work, below the line negative work. The total work bar always matches the change in KE bar.
  • Work done by a constant force: W = F d cosθ, where d is the distance moved and θ is the angle between the force and the direction of motion. F cosθ is the part of the force along the motion.
  • If the force has a part along the motion (θ < 90°), it does positive work: the block speeds up and gains kinetic energy.
  • If the force has a part against the motion (θ > 90°), it does negative work: the block slows down and loses kinetic energy. Friction always acts against the motion, so the work done by friction is always negative; the kinetic energy it removes becomes heat.
  • If the force is at right angles to the motion (θ = 90°), cos 90° = 0, so it does no work. The weight and the normal force are both at right angles to the motion along a level floor, so they never do work here.
  • The block stays at the same height, so its gravitational potential energy does not change. The total work done on the block by all the forces equals the change in its kinetic energy.
