Drag the red handle at the tip of the pull arrow to change how hard, and in which direction, you pull. The ramp is 5 m long from A to B.
The ramp is smooth (no friction), so all the work done by F goes into the box's gravitational potential energy and kinetic energy: W = ΔGPE + ΔKE. Only the part of F along the ramp, F cosθ, does work; the weight and the normal force do no work on the box (the normal force is perpendicular to the motion, and gravity's effect is counted as the GPE). When F cosθ = mg sinα the net force is zero and the box moves up at constant velocity — Tutorial C1: 0 N, 64.3 J, 64.3 J, F = 12.9 N.
