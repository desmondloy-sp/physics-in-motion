Vector triangle method: place the tail of Q at the tip of P; the resultant ΣF (red) joins the tail of P to the tip of Q. Tick the first box to add them in the other order — you get the same resultant (the two triangles make a parallelogram). Measure it the way you would on paper: count boxes for the length, and read the angle from the +x axis.
