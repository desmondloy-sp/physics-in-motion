Tap an appliance to switch it on or off, and set how long it is used each day.
  • Power is the rate of using energy: P = E/t = VI = I2R = V2/R.
  • An appliance's rating (e.g. 2000 W 240 V) is the power it uses when it is connected to its rated voltage.
  • The heating element keeps the same resistance, R = V2/P = 38.4 Ω for the 1500 W 240 V kettle. So on a lower supply voltage it uses less power: at 120 V only a quarter, 375 W.
  • Electricity in Singapore is paid for by the kilowatt-hour. 1 kWh is the energy a 1 kW appliance uses in 1 hour, = 3.6 × 106 J.
  • Energy in kWh = power in kW × time in hours.
